WHO WE ARE

A Trusted Division of Indchemie

DermaCare is the specialized dermatology division of Indchemie Health Specialities Pvt. Ltd., an established pharmaceutical company with over 50 years of excellence and trust in modern healthcare. Born from a profound medical heritage, DermaCare combines the rigorous research discipline of pharmaceutical science with advanced dermatological skin biology.

Our commitment is uncompromising: every solution we produce is clinically tested, dermatologist-formulated, and engineered in WHO-GMP certified facilities. We bridge the gap between medical-grade therapeutics and everyday skincare, delivering results that doctors recommend and patients trust worldwide.

Milestones

Our Journey

A half-century journey of therapeutic excellence, scientific discovery, and unwavering medical dedication.

1947
Founded as Indchemie Health Specialities

1990s
